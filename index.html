01 — ABOUT

Building at the edge of silicon & software

I'm an Electrical Engineer (Computer Systems) graduating from UIT University, Karachi, with a focus on digital IC design — from Verilog RTL and FSMs through FPGA verification to full RTL-to-GDSII ASIC physical design.

My work spans digital design, ASIC physical design, embedded/IoT systems, and applied AI/ML, backed by hands-on experience as an IC Design Trainee running synthesis, static timing analysis, and place-and-route flows on real designs.

I currently work with LibreLane/OpenLane/OpenROAD on the Sky130 PDK, alongside practical experience across FPGA platforms (Zybo, Arty) and Python-based data analysis and computer vision.
